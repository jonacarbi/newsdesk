The idea

The same news, five times. Now once.

One event shows up in dozens of places, each with a different headline. NewsDesk recognises it is the same story and shows it as a single item with every outlet attached.

What is in it

Six categories, one overview

Collects

RSS feeds, Google News, Reddit and Hacker News across six categories: tech, crypto, finance, sport, entertainment and politics.

Clusters

The same event from different outlets becomes one story, with every outlet listed in the detail pane.

Refreshes

Automatically every 30 minutes, or on demand with a shortcut.

Reads

A built-in WebKit reader with history, beside the headline list.

Native and web

A SwiftUI app for macOS 26 and later, with a web dashboard alongside it in the browser.

Studio

Copy-ready post drafts per story or for the top 5, with a Copy button. Posting is manual.

Honest about availability

The app is private.
